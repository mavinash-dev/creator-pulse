# CreatorPulse

> Observability for social media creators — know your numbers, know your worth.

---

## What It Is

CreatorPulse is a social media observability platform for creators. Not just analytics — full observability into your creator business across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and more. Built for India-first, designed to feel like a developer tool, not a social media toy.

**Positioning:** New Relic for creators.

## Why It Exists

Every analytics tool is built for brands. Creators are treated as inventory, not customers. No tool gives creators a real-time, cross-platform view of their performance with alerting, benchmarking, and a shareable live profile — until now.
